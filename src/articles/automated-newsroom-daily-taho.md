Since moving into consulting, my priorities have shifted. I’ve realized that the best way to be empathetic toward my clients' challenges is to double down on being a founder myself.

My project, [Daily Taho](https://www.dailytaho.com), started with a mission: to aggregate Philippine news and make it actually useful. Less celebrity unfollowing dramas, more info that moves the needle on media literacy.

The hurdle? The scale of the Philippine market.

The Philippines is a "super-user" nation. With over 95 million users, our social media adoption rate (nearly 87% of the population) dwarfs the global average of 69%. To make an impact, Daily Taho couldn't just be a website; it had to be an active, social-first engine.

But for a team of one, the "Content Tax" is real. To keep up with the speed of news without losing my mind, I built an automated "newsroom":

- **Make** retrieves the raw news data using an RSS feed.
- **Google Gemini** filters for substance and generates summaries for better clarity.
- **Telegram Messenger** acts as my mobile command center for final review.
- Finally, my brand templates on **Canva** allow me to turn data into social-ready assets in minutes.

My #HotTake: Yes, AI and automation replace parts of a workflow, and they do it with incredible efficiency. But they aren't the "strategy."

Our edge as humans is the ability to stay obsessed with *impact*.

- As a Marketer, my job is to use this thinking to drive sales for my clients.
- As a Founder, my job is to use them to move the needle on media literacy for Filipinos.

Automation handles the speed. I handle the soul.
