Over the past week, I’ve been auditing an ecommerce account and saw a pattern I encounter often:

ROAS was high (5.7x!!), but growth had plateaued. When I dug deeper, I found out that it's mainly due to the following:

- Branded search capturing existing demand
- Returning customers are already familiar with the brand
- Bottom-funnel campaigns are doing the majority of the work

It's not wrong or bad, and the client is actually okay with it. 
But structurally, the business was vulnerable.

This is the difference between managing ads and building a growth system. 

So, what I must accomplish next is to effectively report back to the client the following priorities to sustainably grow:

- Balance demand capture AND demand creation
- Diversify acquisition channels strategically
- Understand where revenue is actually coming from
- Fix the full funnel, not just the ad account

This is the lens I bring when working with DTC brands, not just optimizing campaigns, but diagnosing the entire growth engine.

If you’re running an ecommerce brand and feel like growth has stalled despite “good” performance metrics, let's find time to connect and figure out where the problem lies -- ads, landing pages, or the full system.
