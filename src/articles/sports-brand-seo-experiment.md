I just finished a 14-day experiment with a global sports brand, and the results prove that "small" technical tweaks = big revenue shifts.

![SEO Experiment Results](/assets/projects/sports-brand-seo-experiment.png)

### The Win: 
- 📈 **Search Traffic**: +11.5% 
- 🎯 **CTR**: 2.7% → 3.6% (a 33% increase in clickability!!) 
- 👀 **Impressions**: Slightly decreased

Yup, impressions went down. Yes, that’s a good thing.

It means we stopped shouting at everyone and started speaking to the right someone. By overhauling meta descriptions with a more technical, intentional positioning and sharp CTAs, we transformed scrolling into clicking!

This matters for your brand because:

- **Better CTR** tells Google your content is RELEVANT (hello, better rankings).
- You’re getting **more intentional clicks** from the exact same search volume.
- You’re building **authority** by MATCHING specific user INTENT.
